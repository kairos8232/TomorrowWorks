Three career directions sit directly on a bold route with skill-transfer labels and compact evidence dates. Navigation belongs to the same wayfinding system.
Visual law: deep civic blue main field, turmeric-yellow destination/action, pandan green verified markers, warm ticket-stock ground; confident public signage typography paired with humane reading text; enamel panels, printed route strips, small service-ticket details. One dominant route move.
Constraints: no sidebar, no dashboard, no map of Malaysia, no train photography, no chatbot, no generic centred hero, no equal feature-card row, no invented numbers, no fake testimonials, no AI imagery, no watermark.
